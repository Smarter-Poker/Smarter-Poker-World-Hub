# TRAIN diagnosis of the rejected ROOT replacements

All measurements below use the 88 explicitly admitted TRAIN games, their public ROOT features and teachers, the 42 frozen fold models, and 16 native TRAIN records. No consumed evaluation or protected holdout payload was admitted. This is development diagnosis, not new qualification.

1. **Unsupported regime transfer was admitted as if global rank counts supplied family coverage.** There are only four paired TRAIN signatures. Fold 0 retained two paired signatures; folds 1 and 2 retained three. The largest failure is the held-out high-paired regime: all its rows exceed fitted bounds in several absolute-board-rank features. The independent model puts 96.997% of ROOT mass on the large bet, versus 0.000179% in the teacher; teacher mass is 50.247% small bet and 49.753% check. Its cbet ROOT TV is 0.9726 held out versus 0.0385–0.0436 in the other fitted folds. Paired mean ROOT TV is 0.1562 held out versus 0.0208 fitted. The current admitted public pool supplies no additional paired rank after existing protected and consumed-evaluation fences. The model cannot obtain an unseen-flop certificate from this corpus.

2. **The coupled target discards required conditional information.** At parent small-bet probability zero, `[check,b75,b33*call,b33*fold]` is identical for every response policy. Dividing approximate child masses at inference cannot recover the discarded conditional target when prediction makes that branch reachable. TRAIN check-raise positive conditional loss averages 0.9955bb for coupled versus 0.0260bb for independent. Conditional responses must remain independent ROOT targets, including zero teacher-parent-reach hands. No EV projection or small-probability clipping addresses this information loss.

3. **Teacher drift, action permutation and feature/label row mismatch do not explain these failures.** All 352 ROOT node arrays align with their teacher combo masks and action order. Original and newer native TRAIN teacher frequencies match exactly at all 64 compared node records. The native loader validates all 96 arms. Five-percent clipping makes the worst cbet mismatch larger, not smaller. One-node conditional EV loss is an inadequate proxy for whole-seat best response; the native 3% gate remains necessary.

4. **The historical incumbent comparison favors the incumbent.** It was fitted with historical exposure to the tested ranks and exact TRAIN states. The rejected replacements excluded entire global ranks. Their low fitted errors and much larger held-rank errors expose a transfer/data-support failure, not evidence that ROOT targets inherently fail. Comparisons must disclose exposure and must never call this a final evaluation.

The repair acquires missing public TRAIN support instead of treating an unsupported paired regime as an ordinary prediction: fresh 180bb and 220bb contexts on the same permitted boards bracket each 200bb TRAIN regression game. The 16 exact 200bb games are excluded from fitting. Models use direct conditional frequency targets; a generic support gate requires both depth neighbors, identical other public geometry and absence of the target game from fitting. A second fixed recipe supplies whole-game public ROOT-range distribution features and balances families. Rank exposure is explicit; this tests context interpolation, not unseen-rank extrapolation. Every-seat 3%, strict two-tone and conservative incumbent comparisons remain frozen before new labels or fitting.
