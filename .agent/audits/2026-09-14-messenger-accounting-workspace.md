# Messenger Club And Invoice Workspaces

The live Messenger reopened in a persisted club identity even when a person visited its ordinary social entry. Invoice conversations appeared among ordinary conversations. The notification deep link selected a conversation without loading its messages, leaving a real issued invoice behind an empty chat panel.

This change makes ordinary entry social. Only server-confirmed, active club memberships populate the Club Arena widget. Clicking it selects a club and exposes separate Messages and Invoices tabs. Owning a social page or supplying a club URL does not grant membership, and an ordinary member does not gain a club representative identity. The same authorized workspace serves first load, refresh and search. An accounting classification outage returns an error instead of placing financial conversations in a social list. Account and workspace generations reject delayed responses, including a switch away and back. The former unscoped persisted conversation list is no longer painted during authentication.

Invoice notifications and embedded conversation commands use the same authenticated resolver and message-loading path. Accounting conversations have document cards, current payment status from the linked invoice, and no person-profile or voice/video-call header. Invoice figures remain immutable. Equal-time message pagination now carries the message ID and preserves PostgreSQL microseconds.

The club inbox excludes separate issuer-to-agent invoice threads. Consolidated weekly accounting documents have a dedicated card with rake earned, union receipts and club payouts. Database publication of the consolidated weekly document, treatment of already delivered individual sender receipts, and the original waterfall reconciliation remain in the associated Club Arena accounting work. Their completion is not claimed by this UI change.

Validation: 15 authorization/workspace contracts and 8 rendered/interacted component contracts passed with existing dependencies. The actual helper also read the production account: 8 social conversations with no accounting conversations; Club JAQK ordinary messages with no accounting conversations; two recipient accounting threads in its Invoices tab; the observed invoice notification resolved into that tab. These were read-only service-backed checks, not deployed UI verification. JSX parse and undefined-name/hook checks passed. CI, merge, publisher provenance and a new live browser pass remain required.


Weekly summaries and archive integration: the message API now uses the installed participant-scoped page reader, which verifies invoice provenance and current status and filters archived club payout copies before pagination. The notification feed and list exclude those archived details, and unread counts match the existing rule that neither read flag is true. Database outages no longer pretend to be an empty successful notification list. Genuine human invoice replies remain visible to the issuer in the Invoices tab. Club managers receive one read-only weekly preview while reconciliation is outstanding; individual ledger IDs are omitted, unknown roles stay explicit, and downstream redistribution stays separate. Ordinary players cannot retrieve club totals. Thirty-two native workspace/render checks passed; live read-only helper calls confirmed the existing owner's social inbox contains no accounting threads and club invoices resolve to the separate folder. Two club-summary migrations are installed and 26 PostgreSQL fixture assertions passed. Full deployed browser proof and the financial waterfall reconciliation remain outstanding.
