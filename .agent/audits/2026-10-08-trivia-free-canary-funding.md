# Free tournament qualification funding correction

Original admission records funding_source `none` when the immutable entry fee is zero. The entrant table enforces that equivalence. The Phase 12 validator nevertheless required `player_wallet` for both free and paid humans, so a correctly admitted free entrant could never qualify. The disposable PostgreSQL 17 test reproduced that rejection before the correction.

The forward migration selects `none` for free evidence and `player_wallet` for paid evidence and rejects an unknown paid classification. It preserves active named-wallet authorization at entry time, a genuinely entered human, the exact horse field, terminal zero escrow and reconciliation, and all paid prize and balanced-journal requirements. No wallets, entrants, ledger records, certificates, gates or scheduler state are modified. The installed predecessor hash, sole signature, owner, definer security, empty search path and original owner-only execute ACL are checked. An executable forward rollback restores the predecessor without rewriting evidence.

The existing required Phase 12 PostgreSQL gate uses the actual migration, realistic original free-entry funding, positive free/paid evidence, negative missing/late/blank human and funding/escrow/variance evidence, and an exact rollback/reapply rehearsal. Replica fixtures are not production canaries or launch certification. Actual named-human events and the remaining programme gates still require their own evidence.
