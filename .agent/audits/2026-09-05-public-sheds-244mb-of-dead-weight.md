# 2026-09-05 — public/ sheds 244 MB of dead weight

Raised by Dan while asking why push-to-live is slow on both repos.

## Why public/ is on the critical path at all

Everything under `public/` is cloned, traced, deployed and cached by Vercel on
**every** build, whether or not a byte of it changed. Measured from the build
events of a real production deployment (`dpl_3rxr7mgqCYoa1FeK5j2EXpwRisax`):

| phase | seconds |
| ----- | ------- |
| cloning | 15.1 |
| compile | 30.4 |
| finalizing page optimization | 32.4 |
| collecting build traces | 22.2 |
| deploying outputs | 28.5 |
| creating build cache | 16.8 |
| uploading build cache (644 MB) | 7.4 |

`public/` was **632 MB across 2,357 files**, and `.git` is 4.4 GB.

## What was removed, and how each deletion was proved

**419 files, 244 MB.** `public/` is now 387 MB (−39%).

Nothing was deleted on the strength of a single search. Three independent
checks, and every one of them had to agree:

1. **A URL-path scan of all serving code** — `pages/`, `src/`, `styles/`,
   `next.config.js`, `vercel.json` — matching the file's real serving path, not
   its basename. Basenames collide: `public/images/commander/btn-back.png` and
   `public/images/btn-back.png` are different files and only the second is
   referenced (`pages/terms.js`, `pages/legal/official-rules.js`).
   **The scan carried a control** (`/images/btn-back.png`, known-referenced) so
   that a search which returned nothing because it had been killed could not be
   mistaken for a search that found nothing. It was: two earlier attempts
   returned "0 hits" having been terminated mid-run, and one used `rg`, which is
   not installed on this machine and fails silently.
   **That scan removed 17 files from the delete list** — `images/trivia/lobby-*.jpg`
   and friends, which the audit had classified as superseded. They are live.
2. **The database.** Zero rows in `profiles.avatar_url`,
   `profiles.arena_avatar_url` or `merchandise_items.image_url` point at any
   deleted directory.
3. **The replacement origin, asked directly**, for the two groups that are
   served from somewhere else now:
   - `public/images/commander/**` (93 files, 32 MB): `vercel.json:741` already
     rewrites `/images/commander/:path*` to `commander.smarter.poker`, but
     **Next serves `public/` BEFORE `afterFiles` rewrites**, so the local copies
     were shadowing the remote. **75 of 75 checked return 200 from the remote**,
     so deleting them makes the rewrite live rather than breaking it. (This is
     the same shadowing rule as CLAUDE.md 2.4, one directory over.)
   - `public/images/gto-panels/**` + `public/gto-panels/**` (80 PNGs, 56 MB):
     every game client builds
     `${SUPABASE_URL}/storage/v1/object/public/gto-panels/panels/...` at runtime.
     The `gto-panels` Storage bucket holds **82 objects, 53 MB**, and 78 of the
     81 local files have a name-for-name twin there; the other three are the two
     unreferenced files in the root `public/gto-panels/` staging dir and
     `panel-urls.json`, which is kept as the upload record.

The rest, all with zero references anywhere in either repo:

| group | files | size |
| ----- | ----- | ---- |
| `images/training/*.png` at depth 1 | 137 | 103 MB |
| `images/gto-panels` + `gto-panels` | 80 | 56 MB |

| `videos/*` except `chip-explosion.mp4`, `club-arena-intro.mp4` | 13 | 27 MB |

| `images/news-icons/**` | 17 | 2 MB |
| `images/training/ui` + `images/training/avatars` | 18 | 11 MB |
| root `*-review.html` + `audio-selector.html` | 33 | 1 MB |

Two of those deserve naming. **69 of the 137 training PNGs were literal
`_<13-digit-timestamp>.png` duplicates of the other 68** — 52 MB of pure
duplication left by `scripts/copy-training-images.js`, a one-off importer. And
the 33 `*-review.html` files were **dev artifacts publicly reachable on
smarter.poker**, generated by `scripts/generate_master_html.js` and linked from
nowhere.

## Deliberately NOT removed

- **`images/trivia` depth 1 (2.9 MB).** The scan proved the audit wrong about
  this directory once. It is small, and being wrong about it twice is not worth
  three megabytes.
- **~49 MB of design-iteration leftovers** (`vip-card-{clean,trimmed,v2..v7}`,
  `jarvis-*`, `header-*-{clean,trimmed}`, `smarter-poker-logo-nobg{2..10}`).
  Each needs a two-second human eyeball, and the safe path is staged removal
  with a week of watching 404s — not a bulk delete at the end of a session.
  **`images/global-header/global-header-approved-source.png` is in that set and
  must not be touched without reading Club Arena CLAUDE.md §10.7 first** — it is
  name-adjacent to the approved-artwork law that the hamburger revert war ran on.
- **The 478 MB of PNGs with no modern-format sibling.** `avatars/vip/*.png`
  alone is 86.6 MB at an average of 1.14 MB per avatar, all of it genuinely
  referenced. That is a conversion job — `avatars/table/` already proves the
  pattern works here — and it is three times the size of everything removed
  above. It is the next piece, not this one.

## Three "dangling references" - and what they actually turned out to be

An earlier draft of this note reported three broken-image references as live
production defects. **That was wrong on all three, and this is the correction.**
Each was checked properly afterwards:

1. **`/merch/*.jpg` on five `merchandise_items` rows: NOT a defect.**
   `MerchStore.jsx` carries an explicit `UNSHIPPED_MERCH_IMAGES` set naming
   exactly those paths, with the reasoning above it: "These paths were seeded
   before their product photography was shipped. Let the card render its
   deliberate category placeholder immediately instead of issuing a guaranteed
   404." Somebody had already thought about this and handled it well. Nothing to
   fix, and "fixing" it would have been damage.

2. **`/cards/backs/${style}.png` in `src/lib/poker-engine/CardAssets.js`: dead
   code, now deleted.** The live helper is `src/lib/CardAssets.js`, which
   returns `/images/card-backs/<color>.jpg` - and those files exist. Nothing
   imported the poker-engine copy, not even the test sitting two directories
   below it, which requires `../../CardAssets` (the live one). Note the
   surrounding subsystem IS live: `pages/api/poker/*` imports GameController,
   RateLimiter, AntiCheat and authMiddleware from `src/lib/poker-engine/`.
   Only that one file was orphaned, and only that one file was removed.

3. **`/avatars/avatar1..10.png` in `LeaderboardWidget.tsx`: dead code, now
   deleted.** Nothing in the repo imported the component or its
   `generateMockLeaderboard`, which invents fifteen players ('PokerShark99',
   'BluffMaster', 'TheNit'), assigns them `Math.random()` scores and random
   up/down trends. No player has ever seen it. It is deleted rather than left
   in place because the next agent to search for a leaderboard would find a
   ready-made one that fabricates its rows, and the real leaderboards
   (`LeaderboardDisplay`, `/api/poker/leaderboards`) are elsewhere.

The lesson is the one this whole audit keeps repeating: a reference that looks
dangling is a hypothesis, not a finding. Two of these were in code nothing
runs, and the third was already solved by someone who left a comment saying so.

## Still open, and it is Dan's call

Those same five legacy merch rows - Stealth and Gold Card Protector ($24.99),
100-Chip Travel Set ($79.99), 500-Chip Pro Set ($199.99), Premium Playing Cards
($14.99) - are `is_active = true`, carry stock, have **no Printful mapping**,
and have **never been ordered** (0 orders each). Every other item in the store
is Printful-fulfilled.

They are not a money-integrity hazard: `purchase-with-diamonds` finds no
mapping, sets `automaticFulfillment = false`, and records the order as
`fulfillmentMode: 'manual'`. That is a deliberate path, not a leak. But it does
mean a player can spend up to 19,999 diamonds on a chip set with no photograph
and no supplier behind it, and the only thing standing between that and a
support ticket is that nobody has tried in a month.

Left alone on purpose. CLAUDE.md 10.6 gives agents the money decisions on PAST
events and reserves "what is sold, and at what price" for Dan. Deactivating five
live products is merchandising, not correction. **Recommendation: deactivate
them until photography and a supplier exist, or point them at real products.**
One `update merchandise_items set is_active = false` and it is done.

## Hardening

**`CHECK 21: public/ stays inside its budget`** in `build-safety-gate.yml`,
backed by `scripts/ci/check-public-budget.mjs`. A ratchet in the same shape as
CHECK 16: a pull request may not exceed the baseline, and when you remove
weight you lower the baseline in the same commit so the room cannot be silently
refilled. On failure it prints the ten largest files and the two cheaper
answers — a modern format, or an origin that is not this build. It also emits a
notice when the gap grows past 30 MB, so the ratchet gets tightened instead of
quietly becoming slack.

**`scripts/prune-stale-worktrees.sh`, ported from Club Arena.** It existed in
only one of the seven repos, and the World Hub's worktrees are the larger ones.
This was not academic: the Mac was at **239 MiB free of 926 GiB** when this work
started, with single World Hub trees at 5.1 GB and 4.7 GB, and the checkout for
this very change failed with `No space left on device`. Running the Club Arena
copy reclaimed **16 GB**; nothing had ever been able to touch the World Hub's.
The script only removes a worktree that is clean, pushed, and idle for 72 hours,
and uses `git worktree remove` without `--force` as the final guard.

Two things it correctly refused and a human should look at: **18 Club Arena
worktrees hold commits that exist nowhere else**, and 40 are dirty.
