# The Marketplace Restores Content Hierarchy (2026-09-30)

The remaining Marketplace pages now follow the cleaner Page 1 direction
without copying Page 1 into every route. Existing layouts and commerce
flows remain intact while oversized decorative housings, empty ornament and
flat generic controls give way to restrained chrome, blue depth, readable
cards and clearer action hierarchy.

## Restored Pages

- VIP Membership, Compare Plans and Manage Membership
- Official Merch, Product Details and Fulfillment
- Smarter Rewards and Reward Details
- Club Shop and Item Details
- Cart, Wishlist, Order History and Order Receipts

The approved gold VIP artwork remains unchanged. The Club Shop continues to
offer one All Throwables Pack with composite gameplay artwork, not separate
purchases for individual throwable types.

## What Stayed Intact

Pricing, catalog authority, account ownership, authentication, checkout,
payment, refund, order and entitlement behavior were preserved. Navigation
continues in the same browser tab. The restored surfaces use readable
content-first panels, accessible controls and one non-obstructive footer
without changing the locked Diamond Marketplace Page 1.

## Verification

The final local Marketplace gate passed 69 of 69 pretests and 609 of 609
canonical tests. The tested main desktop and mobile routes had no
horizontal document overflow. These are local candidate results, not a
claim of production publication.

## Still Deferred

Printful remains intentionally disconnected until the fulfillment account
and production catalog are ready. The proposed Lifetime VIP policy that
would issue 2,000 Diamonds on the first of each month and expire unused
free grants after 90 days also remains separate policy and implementation
work. Neither deferred item was silently added by this visual restoration.
