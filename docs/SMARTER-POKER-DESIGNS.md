# #SMARTER.POKER Designs

This is the permanent visual direction for Smarter.Poker product and marketing surfaces.

## Core Direction

- Build cinematic, high-detail interfaces with the presence of premium game hardware.
- Use physical depth: layered metal, bevels, inset panels, dimensional highlights, reflections, and controlled glow.
- Favor deep navy and black foundations with chrome, cyan, electric blue, and restrained violet accents.
- Keep silhouettes and corners sharp. Do not round cards, panels, buttons, navigation, or frames.
- Avoid generic gradients, flat template cards, soft SaaS styling, and interchangeable AI-looking layouts.
- Use original 3D poster art as a focal layer, then support it with disciplined typography and spacing.

## Typography And Copy

- Capitalize The First Letter Of Every Word In Visible Interface Copy.
- Use condensed display type for major headlines and a technical sans serif for supporting text.
- Keep hierarchy decisive: eyebrow, headline, support copy, then action.
- Keep important numbers centered and unobstructed. Never place package values over product art.

## Components

- Frames should feel machined, with visible top-edge light and lower-edge shadow.
- Buttons should resemble precise hardware controls, with sharp corners and a clear pressed state.
- Cards should have distinct art, a centered information zone, and an isolated action footer.
- Navigation should read as one continuous control rail instead of a row of floating pills.
- Glows should reveal structure and depth, not substitute for structure.
